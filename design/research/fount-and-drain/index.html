Fount and drain

netties, 15–16 October 2026. An experiment in showing a port's direction on its own, with no port or wire to point an arrow along: a fount for a port that gives, a drain for one that takes. Not used in the end; kept for reference.

The idea

The user's drawings. Giving: "circles moving outward, becoming thinner and fainter as they go. Like dropping a pebble in a lake." Taking: "a circle moves inwards, contracting and becoming more angular as it does so, as parts of the circle collapse inwards. Like water draining down a hole." The hope was that seeing these wherever directions came up would make them familiar by the time they mattered.

How it went

  1. Three rings each, a fixed rim for the drain with rings turning as they shrank. Too small to read: "they just kinda look like circles". The panel re-rendered on every pointer move, restarting the animation, so the fount looked still; fixed by putting every icon on one clock. (cfb918a)
  2. The drain's rim itself creasing inward, without turning (that "becomes a pretty flower ... doesn't illustrate draining"). Made lopsided to match the drawing, which wasn't wanted: the drawing was only hand-drawn. (4161dcb)
  3. Symmetric creases, valleys falling faster than tips; each new rim fading in slowly, like a Shepard tone, so it feels ever-falling rather than pulsing outwards. Rays on the fount, so it reads as sending out even when still. But the faint rim never looked like a circle, the star faded out too visibly, and the fount became a sun with its moving rings too small to see. (270262e)
  4. This page: the drain comes in as a full circle and creases only once inside, and thins and shrinks to nothing instead of fading; the fount's rings use the whole icon while moving, with rays only when still. (c9946a7)
